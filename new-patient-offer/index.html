Before you reserve

Straight answers to common questions.

What happens after I submit the form?
We save your contact details and preferred appointment window first. You are then taken to our live booking calendar to choose the exact day and time that works for you.
What is included in the $49 new-patient visit?
The offer includes a consultation with your doctor, a focused chiropractic examination, digital X-rays and thermal imaging when clinically appropriate, and a report of findings with clear recommendations.
Will I be adjusted on the first visit?
Not always. Your first visit is primarily about evaluation and clarity. In some cases gentle care may begin after the exam, but only when your doctor determines it is appropriate and you understand the recommendation.
Do you accept insurance?
Yes, Life Charge accepts most major medical insurance plans. The $49 promotion is a separate new-patient offer and cannot be combined with insurance. Our team can explain both options before your visit.
Where is Life Charge Chiropractic?
The office is at 1921 Nashville Pike, Suite 500, Gallatin, TN 37066. It is easy to reach from Gallatin, Hendersonville, and the surrounding Sumner County communities.
